2-door vs 4-door: what changes with grab handles?

2-door Bronco: Two front roll bar positions. A 2-pack covers both front passengers. If you never carry rear passengers, a 2-pack is enough.

4-door Bronco: Four roll bar positions. A 2-pack only covers front passengers — rear passengers get nothing. Always buy a 4-pack for the 4-door. All of the picks above come in 4-packs.
